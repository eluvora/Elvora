WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Help & Support

A few things to know about this version of ELVORA.

Is ELVORA free?

All features in this starter website are available without a subscription or usage counter.

Does it use AI?

No. The current tools work locally in your browser and do not generate AI answers.

Where is my data saved?

Notes, cards and other saved items use browser local storage on this device. They are not synced between devices.

Do I need an account?

No account or login is required in this starter version.
